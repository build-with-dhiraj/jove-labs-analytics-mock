How we count
Trials on this tab
Labs PI trials open at some point in the period: active on its last day, or ended inside it.
PIs in trial now
the PI's 45-day Labs trial window is open today.
Trainees and co-trainers covered
members of that PI's labs, whose access runs on the PI's trial.
Trial PIs who asked for a demo
PIs with a Labs trial who have ever requested a demo.
Calendar demo booked
the PI picked a slot with the account manager.
Trial trainees who recommended JoVE to their library
trainees on a Labs trial whose e-mail submitted a Recommend to Your Library form after their trial began.
New subscription after trial
The PI's institution started a paid subscription after the PI's Labs trial began.
Labs views made on trial, Labs views during trial
views of the PI's labs while the trial window was open. This is Total Trial Views in Detailed reports, JoVE Labs Analysis, summed over every lab; that table's summary bar adds up only the labs its filters list.
Reminder e-mail sent
the trial-ending e-mail, sent 14 days before the window closes.
Active, Ending in 14 days
personal trials running today, with more than 14 days left or with 14 days or fewer.
